CSV format and scoring rules
Headers accepted: symbol,timeframe,period,trades,return,maxdd,pf,benchmark,avghold,oos,exits.
  • Under 30 trades is flagged as a weak sample; 100+ is preferable when strategy frequency allows.
  • One market is never treated as proof that an edge transfers.
  • If exits stopped before the test ended, the audit is automatically rejected.
  • An out-of-sample result means data that was not inspected while building or tuning the strategy.
